01Introduction

TIIX SQL Studio is an AI-powered SQL assistant that helps people explore business databases using natural language. This policy describes how the application handles information when you use it.

It applies to this TIIX SQL Studio deployment and is written to be understandable rather than legalistic. It is not a substitute for legal advice, and it should be reviewed by the operator before the deployment is used with data that is subject to specific legal requirements.

02Information that may be processed

Depending on how the deployment is configured and used, the following may be processed:

  • Your questions. The natural-language prompts you type into the studio.
  • Conversation context. Recent questions and the SQL previously generated in a session, used to understand follow-up questions.
  • Database structure. Read-only metadata such as table names, column names, types and detected relationships. This does not include the values stored in your rows.
  • Query results. Rows returned by read-only queries, shown in your browser.
  • Uploaded files. If you import a database, SQL script or spreadsheet, the file is processed to make its contents queryable in a sandbox.
  • Access information. An email address, only if a visitor submits one through the access request form.

03How information is used

Information is used only to provide and operate the assistant, including to:

  • turn your question into SQL and an explanation;
  • run the generated query against the connected database;
  • display results and optional visualizations;
  • keep a short conversation context for follow-up questions;
  • protect the service, such as through rate limiting and abuse prevention.

The application does not sell your information, and it does not use your business data to train its own models.

04Database & business data

TIIX connects to a database that the operator controls. Generated queries are read-only: the application is designed to run SELECT statements and does not modify your data. Query results are returned to the browser that asked for them.

Uploaded files are handled in a sandboxed area and used to build a queryable copy. Because databases can contain personal or sensitive information, you should only connect or upload data that you are authorised to use, and you should understand what that data contains.

05AI processing

When you ask a question, the assistant sends a request to an external AI provider to generate SQL and an explanation. That request can include your question, recent conversation context, and the database's structural metadata (table and column names and types). It does not include the values stored in your rows, because the assistant generates SQL before any query is run.

AI-generated output can be wrong. Generated SQL and insights may contain errors, may misinterpret your question, or may not apply to your situation. Review them before relying on them for important business decisions.

06Third-party services

The assistant relies on third-party AI infrastructure to generate SQL. Depending on configuration, requests may be sent to an external AI provider such as NVIDIA's API or Google Gemini. This means that the parts of a request described in the AI processing section above are transmitted to infrastructure outside this deployment, and are handled according to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

This deployment does not claim that data is never sent to third parties. It is transparent that AI processing may involve third-party infrastructure. The operator of a deployment is responsible for reviewing each provider's terms before use.

07Logs & technical information

The application and its hosting platform may record technical information such as request timestamps, a request identifier, status codes, durations and error traces. These records are used to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.

Sensitive values are not written to logs by the application: API keys, passwords and access credentials are redacted or omitted. Logs are retained by the hosting platform according to its own settings.

08Cookies & session information

TIIX uses a small number of first-party cookies to make the studio work. These are session cookies, not advertising cookies:

  • Authentication session cookie. Set for an owner sign-in, so the browser can prove its session without exposing the secret to scripts. It is HttpOnly and not readable by JavaScript.
  • Access and trial cookies. Used to recognise a visitor's access session and to track free trial attempts.
  • Theme preference. Your dark or light choice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage), not sent to the server.

No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are set by the application.

09Data retention

Conversation sessions and related metadata are stored for as long as they are needed to provide the session history you may rely on, and can be deleted from within the studio. Uploaded sandbox files remain until they are removed. Trial and access cookies expire according to their configured lifetimes.

On hosted deployments, the underlying platform may keep logs and backups for its own retention period. Where the application stores data on a read-only or ephemeral filesystem, that data is not expected to persist.

10Data security

The application is built with security-conscious choices: generated SQL is read-only by construction, credentials are kept on the server and compared in constant time, sensitive values are not returned to the browser or written to logs, and request rate limits are applied to costly operations.

No system can be described as perfectly secure. Reasonable measures are taken, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed, and you should not connect data whose exposure would be unacceptable without first evaluating the deployment yourself.

11User rights

Depending on where you are and on applicable law, you may have rights regarding information about you, such as requesting access to it, correcting it, or asking for it to be deleted. Some of these can be exercised directly in the studio, for example by deleting a session.

To make a request, use the contact details in the final section of this policy. This policy does not itself create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.

12Third-party links

The interface may reference external services or documentation. This privacy policy does not cover those external sites, and the application is not responsible for their content or practices. Review their own privacy policies before using them.

13Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or to how it handles information. When it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the assistant after an update means the revised policy applies.
