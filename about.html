About TIIX SQL Studio

TIIX SQL Studio is an AI-powered SQL assistant designed to make business data easier to understand for people who are not SQL experts. Ask a question in plain language, and TIIX turns it into a read-only SQL query, explains what it did, and presents the answer in a form you can act on.

Natural language questions Read-only SQL Explainable results

The problem

Small businesses and shops often hold valuable sales, inventory, product and financial data. But that data usually lives in a database that only speaks SQL, and getting a useful answer from it can require technical knowledge or a developer's help. The information is there — the ability to ask it questions is not.

The solution

TIIX lets you ask questions about your business data in natural language and receive useful, SQL-driven insights without writing SQL by hand. The assistant inspects the database structure, generates a safe read-only query, shows you the SQL it produced, and explains the result in plain English so you can decide what it means.

What TIIX focuses on

Simplicity

Ask a question the way you would ask a colleague, and get an answer without learning a query language first.

Business insights

Move from raw tables to answers about sales, stock, products and finances that help you make everyday decisions.

SQL assistance

TIIX generates the SQL for you, shows it openly, and explains it, so the query is never a black box.

Data awareness

The assistant works from the actual structure of your database, so questions and answers stay grounded in your real tables and columns.

Security-conscious design

Queries are read-only by construction and sensitive configuration stays on the server. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.

For non-technical users

TIIX is built so that shop owners, operators and analysts can understand their data without becoming database specialists.

Built for practical business intelligence

TIIX SQL Studio is meant for the real, everyday questions a business asks of its own data — not for a perfect analytics platform. It favours clarity over jargon, shows the SQL it writes, and keeps a short conversation context so follow-up questions feel natural. The goal is simple: help you understand what your data is telling you and move forward with confidence.
